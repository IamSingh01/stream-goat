> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Android: uninstall the old version before installing 10.0.0 (one last time).**
> This version can't install over 9.x: Android refuses an update signed differently from the copy you have.
> 1. In the old app: **Settings → Updates and backup → Back up** (or back up to Google Drive).
> 2. Uninstall Stream Goat.
> 3. Install 10.0.0, then **Settings → Updates and backup → Restore**. Your extensions download again by themselves,
>    and the app restarts to finish.
>
> From the next version on, updates install over the old one from inside the app (Settings → Updates and backup):
> no uninstalling, and your extensions, settings, history and downloads stay.

## What's new in 10.0.0

**Updates inside the app**

- New versions **download and install from inside the app**, with a progress bar (Android asks once to allow
  installing from Stream Goat). A **Test builds** switch gets the newest test versions.
- A fresh install starts with **Live TV playlists**: iptv-org for your country, iptv-org (all countries) and Free-TV.

**Smoother on TV (Google TV, Android TV, projectors)**

- The TV gets the **tablet layout**: more episodes and posters per row, nothing cut off, the side bar fits.
- **Remote like the official app**: the selected title slides to the start of its row, the selected row comes to the
  middle of the screen, a row is entered at its first title, the Home banner hides fully when you move down.
- Lighter: the banner no longer loads every title the remote passes over; the poster zoom is drawn, not rebuilt.
- **Holding OK** on a title opens its menu (Mark as watched, Remove, Hide…) and waits for you to choose.
- **Mouse** (air mouse, Bluetooth mouse): one click opens, hold and drag a row sideways, ‹ › buttons on a row.
- Home's banner is a **rounded card** below the top bar; coming back from a title keeps your place on Home.

**Players**

- **ExoPlayer** (the official app's player) joins **VLC**: pick one per kind of source (extensions, Stremio,
  torrents, Live TV, files) in Settings → Player → Player engine, switch while watching, or be asked every time. If
  one can't play a source, the other is tried. NetMirror plays with its real length, starts fast and seeks properly.
- Volume boost above 100 %, 5.1 surround passthrough, buffering limits for slow internet or old devices, and a
  "your internet is slow" hint.
- A source that crashed a player is played with the other one next time.

**New sections**

- **Sports**: live scores for football, cricket, basketball, American football, tennis, hockey, MMA, baseball, F1
  and golf: a live banner, Live now, Coming up, Results, and sports news you read in a pop-up.
- **YouTube**: YouTube's TV app with ads removed and sign-in with a code (made for TV; on phones it shows a note,
  and the tab can be hidden in Settings → Appearance → Tabs).
- **Music** in the style of YouTube Music: Home, Explore, Library, moods, playlists, liked songs, Up next and
  recommendations. On tablets and TVs the player sits beside the list. Music stops when you leave its section.

**Fixes and more**

- **Skip intro** works for anime that extensions list as series (MovieBox, Cinemeta…).
- **MovieBox** hides adult titles while adult (NSFW) content is off.
- Back from the player returns to where you were (Downloads, Live TV group, Settings section).
- Live TV keeps the country you picked.
- Extensions that open their own dialogs (CNC Verse) load properly; a broken extension download is replaced by
  pressing Reinstall.
- Restoring a backup asks to restart the app, so everything comes back.
- WeTrakr two-way sync and app logs (Settings → App logs). The built-in web browser was removed (YouTube has its place).

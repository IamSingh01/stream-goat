> **Updating from 10.0.x:** install over the old version; everything stays. Android and the computer apps also offer it
> inside the app (Settings → Updates and backup). If Google Play Protect blocks the install or the update, see
> [Play Protect](https://github.com/sherazi5110/stream-goat#android-phones-tablets-and-android-tv).

## What's new in 11.0.0

**Watched, everywhere**

- A big **WATCHED** stamp on posters, and **Continue** / **New episodes** badges on shows: matched by title across
  every extension, so a film watched in one extension shows as watched in all of them.
- A film or episode counts as watched from **80%** (its tick, the stamp, gone from Continue Watching).
- A weekly show isn't marked WATCHED while its extension already has more episodes than have aired.
- **WeTrakr**: two-way sync; *Sync now* imports your whole watch history (it couldn't before).

**One Google Drive backup for all your devices**

- Phone, TV, Windows, Mac and Linux share **one backup** and stay **in sync**: My list, history, where you stopped,
  watched marks, extensions, Live TV and playlists. Changes from each device are merged, not overwritten; each device
  keeps its own settings. The old one-per-device backups are merged in and moved to Drive's trash.

**TV remote (Android TV, Google TV, Fire TV)**

- The selection no longer jumps away (to the Home icon, the top bar or the Music tabs) when a list changes, a button
  changes or a panel closes.
- **Back** returns to the same tab and item (play from Downloads, Back: Downloads, same download), one step at a time
  (Settings section → section list → Home), and closes a player panel with **one** press.
- Every button can be reached: delete a repository, a download or a backup, remove a recent search, a song's menu,
  the music seek bar.
- Player panels (Sources, Subtitles, Audio…) open on what's chosen now and return to their button.

**Player**

- **Sound on every phone**: ExoPlayer plays **Dolby (DDP 5.1), DTS and TrueHD** with FFmpeg's decoders where the phone
  has none (films played silently before).
- **Subtitle style** works with ExoPlayer (NetMirror and most extensions) and mpv, with **Apply** and **Done** buttons.
- Subtitles downloaded from **OpenSubtitles** show (the video's own track stayed on before).

**Fixes**

- Home says why it's empty (the extension's site down, or its catalogs off) instead of a black page.
- Windows/Mac/Linux Live TV: checking a big channel list (All IPTV) no longer freezes the window.
- Android updates: says when Google Play Protect blocks an update; test builds know exactly which build is installed.

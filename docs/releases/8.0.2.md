## What's new in 8.0.2

**Live TV works better with big public playlists** (iptv-org, free-codecs and similar):

- **Every channel shows.** Android's TV guide stopped at 400 channels and its group buttons at 40. They now go on as
  far as your lists do. (The Windows/Linux guide still asks you to narrow down with a group or a search past 250
  channels, and says so.)
- **Channels in several groups are under each one.** A playlist's `group-title="Animation;Kids"` used to count only as
  Animation, so in a **kids profile** some children's channels were missing. Now it's under both, and the cards show
  "Animation · Kids".
- **Check channels.** A new button on the Live TV page opens every channel's stream (12 at a time) and tells you which
  ones answer. It also follows HLS lists into their first quality, because a list often still answers when the video
  behind it is gone. Channels that don't answer are dimmed, with the reason ("gone (HTTP 404)", "no answer",
  "security certificate problem"…), and **Hide not working** leaves them out until the next check.
- **Backup addresses.** When a channel doesn't play, the player tries the same channel from your other playlists
  (matched by its tvg-id, where @SD and @HD count as one channel, or by name without quality marks). Its Sources
  menu lists them as "Backup from …".
- **Better playlist reading.**
  - Headers given as attributes on the channel line (`http-referrer`, `http-user-agent`, `http-origin`) are now used.
  - Lines a converter broke ("…like Gecko) Chrome/120…" left in the line) are repaired, so the channel gets the
    browser identity its server expects.
  - Channel names containing a comma ("Love Nature, 4K") are kept whole.
- **Android: choose a Live TV source** at the top of the Live TV page ("All sources ▾"), as on Windows and Linux:
  one playlist at a time, or all of them, plus Refresh and Live TV sources.
- **Android: add a playlist from a file.** Settings → Live TV → M3U playlist → **Choose a file** (.m3u, .m3u8). The
  file is copied into the app, so it keeps working if the original is moved.

**Report problems on GitHub.** After a crash, Android's report has a **Report on GitHub** button: GitHub's new-issue
page opens with the report filled in, and you press Submit (a GitHub account is needed). On a TV it shows a QR code to
open the page on your phone. Settings → About → **Report a problem** does the same without a crash, on Android,
Windows and Linux. Nothing is sent by the app itself.

**Choosing sources (Android)**

- **The sources list opens from the bottom, full width,** with every source's **whole name** (no more "…"), a
  **search box** ("hindi", "1080", a server's name) and **chips** for the languages, audio (Dual / Multi audio) and
  qualities found in the names: tap Hindi to see only Hindi sources. The one playing has a check mark.
- **Settings → Player → When you press Play:** *Start the best source automatically* or *Let me choose the source*.
  With "Let me choose", the list opens by itself and fills in as sources are found.
- **Quality inside a source.** When one source carries several qualities (an HLS list with 1440p … 480p), a
  **Quality** button in the player offers Auto or a fixed one, as official CloudStream does. It keeps your place, and
  keeps the audio languages when the audio is separate from the video.
- **Extensions page:** the two kinds are one switch, and the buttons and sections have room between them.
- **Online subtitles download again (Android).** Picking one in Subtitles → Search online said "Couldn't download:
  rememberCoroutineScope left the composition": the menu closed and cancelled its own download. The player downloads
  it now.
- **Download → choose a source shows whole names (Android)**, larger, with the same search box and language /
  quality chips as the player's Sources, so you can tell the Hindi, English or Tamil versions apart.

**Torrents (Android)**

- **Torrentio and other torrent add-ons.** Their torrent streams used to be dropped, so Torrentio showed nothing. Now:
  - **With a debrid service** (Real-Debrid, AllDebrid, Premiumize, TorBox…): set Torrentio up at
    torrentio.strem.fun/configure and install that link. Its streams are ordinary links and play like any other.
  - **Torrent streaming** (Settings → Player → Torrents, **off by default**): torrent sources (🧲) play while they
    download. The video file is fetched in order, start and end first, and seeking fetches the part you jump to.
    The loading screen shows peers, speed and buffering, and the file is deleted when you stop. Turning it on first
    explains that your device uploads to others and that your IP address is visible to them, and uploading is
    limited (50 KB/s to unlimited, 100 KB/s by default). Direct links are still tried before torrents.
  - When only torrents were found and torrent streaming is off, the player says so and how to get them.

**Player suggestions from [issue #36](https://github.com/sherazi5110/stream-goat/issues/36) (Android)**

- **Resize in one tap:** the resize button now switches Fit → Fill → Stretch directly. Original, 16:9 and 4:3 are in
  Settings → Player → Picture size.
- **Rotate button** (phones and tablets): landscape, portrait (for vertical videos and shorts) or as the device is
  held. Your choice is remembered.
- **Picture in picture:** the video keeps playing in a small window over other apps.
- **Your audio language first:** pick languages with chips (Hindi, English, Urdu, Tamil…) in Settings → Player.
  Sources whose names say that language come first, and the matching audio track is chosen. Subtitles have the same
  chips.
- **Even out loud and quiet sounds:** a volume leveler (quieter explosions and music, clearer voices), in the
  player's Audio menu and in Settings → Player.
- **Quality inside a source** is the Quality button above.

**Android TV with a mouse**

- **The first click selects, the second opens.** On a TV, clicking a poster, Continue Watching card, episode or Live
  TV channel highlights it (and shows it in the banner); clicking it again opens it. The remote's OK still opens at
  once, and buttons still work with one click.
- **Rows scroll ahead:** the selected poster stays about one card away from the row's edge, so selecting the last
  one you can see (with a click or the remote) brings the next ones into view.
- **Player:** a click on the picture shows the controls and settings buttons; another click hides them.

**Subtitles in the player (Android)**

- **Sync subtitles:** Subtitles → Sync subtitles moves them earlier or later (0.1 s, 1 s and 5 s steps) until they
  match the voices. It changes while the video keeps playing.
- **Sync audio:** Audio → Sync audio does the same for the sound when voices don't match the lips.
- **Subtitle style:** Subtitles → Subtitle style has size, text colour, font, bold, italic, all capitals, edge
  (outline, shadow, both or none), outline size and colour, a background box with its colour and opacity, height on
  screen and alignment, with a live preview. The video reopens where it was when you close the panel.
- **Remove closed captions** ("[door knocks]", "(laughs)", ♪ lyrics, "NAME:" labels) and **remove ads**
  ("Subtitles by…", website lines) from subtitle files. Remove ads is on by default.
- Subtitles built into the video keep their own text: italic, capitals and the clean-ups apply to subtitle files
  (from the extension, OpenSubtitles or translated).

**New navigation look (all apps)**

- **Phones:** the bottom bar is now a floating rounded pill. The page you're on shows as a lighter pill with its
  name; the others are icons, with Settings after a thin line.
- **Tablets, TVs and Windows/Linux:** the side bar is a vertical rounded pill, centred beside the page, with the
  logo above it and your profile below.
- **Web version:** the same floating pill on phones, and the top links (Home, Series, Movies, My List, Live TV)
  sit in a rounded pill on computers.

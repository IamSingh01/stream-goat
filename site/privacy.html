Stream Goat

Privacy policy

Stream Goat, by Syed Ali Hamza · Effective 15 November 2026

Stream Goat is a free, open-source app for Windows, Linux, Android and Android TV. It has no accounts of its own, no advertising and no analytics or tracking. The developer runs no server that receives your data.

What stays on your device

Your settings, profiles (and their PINs), My list, watch history and progress, recent searches, repositories, installed extensions and their settings, Live TV sources, Stremio add-ons and downloads are stored on your device only. If the app closes because of an error, a report of it is kept on your device and is shared only if you choose to copy or share it.

Optional: Google Drive backup

If you choose Sign in with Google (Settings → Updates and backup), Stream Goat asks Google for permission to use your Google Drive's hidden app data folder (the drive.appdata permission). With it, the app:

  • stores one backup file there for each of your devices that is signed in: your settings, profiles, My list, history and progress, repositories, the list of extensions and their settings, Live TV sources and Stremio add-ons, as listed above; it is updated every 5 minutes when something changed;
  • reads these files back when you restore (for example your phone's backup on your TV), deletes one when you ask it to, and shows the email address of the account you signed in with;
  • cannot see, open or change any other file in your Google Drive.

The backup goes directly between your device and Google; the developer never receives it or your sign-in. The sign-in is kept only on the device you signed in on and is never part of the backup. Stream Goat does not use this data for anything else, does not sell or share it, and does not use it for advertising.

Stream Goat's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

To remove it: choose Sign out in the app (this also withdraws the app's access); delete the backup in Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → Stream Goat → Delete hidden app data; and you can withdraw access at any time in your Google Account → Security → Third-party apps & services.

Extensions and add-ons

What you watch comes from extensions and add-ons that you choose to install; they are made by others, not by Stream Goat. When you browse, search or play, they contact their own websites, which receive those requests and your IP address under their own privacy policies.

Other services the app contacts

To show posters, details, subtitles and updates, the app contacts services such as GitHub (app updates, repositories), Cinemeta and OpenSubtitles (via Stremio's servers), TMDB, AniList, AniSkip, Google Translate (only when you translate subtitles) and the DNS service you pick in Settings. They receive the request (for example a title you looked up) and your IP address. The app sends them no account or personal details.

Add from phone

On Android TV, Add from phone opens a small page on your home network only, protected by a code shown on the TV, and only while that screen is open. Nothing goes over the internet.

Children

Kids profiles limit what can be seen in the app. Stream Goat collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page. Questions: open an issue at github.com/sherazi5110/stream-goat/issues.
